# Important Notice: v2.0-hotfix Update

I thank all researchers, practitioners, and readers for your interest in the **Harmonic Phase Transformer for Financial Prediction** project.  
Your engagement drives me to uphold the highest standards of reproducibility and scientific integrity.

This notice clarifies critical updates made in **v2.0-hotfix** that address data leakage issues present in earlier versions.  
**Please read carefully before using or citing this repository.**

---

## What Was Fixed?

| Issue | Previous (v1.x) | Fixed (v2.0-hotfix) |
|-------|----------------|----------------------|
| **Window Construction** | Windows built across all assets → cross‑ticker leakage (e.g., AMZN windows influenced TSLA predictions). | Windows built **independently per asset** – no cross‑ticker contamination. |
| **Scaling** | `StandardScaler` fitted on the **entire dataset** (including validation/test periods) before splitting. | Scaler fitted **exclusively on the training split** – no forward‑looking information. |
| **Results** | Accuracies artificially inflated (often >70%) due to leakage. | Results are **honest and reproducible**: coverage drops to 2.5%–18.8%, accuracies vary across assets (25%–100% on selected trades). |
